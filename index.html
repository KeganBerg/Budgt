FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to pay monthly?

No. Budgt is built around having no subscription. You won't be asked to start a trial or upgrade to a paid tier.

What devices does it work on?

Budgt is a web app, so it runs in any modern browser on your laptop, tablet or phone. There's nothing to install.

Can I change my dashboard later?

Any time. Add, remove and rearrange widgets whenever your priorities change.
