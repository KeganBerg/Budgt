FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to pay monthly?

No. Budgt is built around having no subscription. You won't be asked to start a trial or upgrade to a paid tier.

What devices does it work on?

Budgt is a web app, so it runs in any modern browser on your laptop, tablet or phone. There's nothing to install.

What's a savings outlook?

Budgt looks at your income, spending and debts to show whether you're on pace for each savings goal, and roughly when you'll reach it.

Why itemize receipts?

A single "Groceries $142" line hides a lot. Itemizing shows where small purchases add up, which is where most easy savings are.
